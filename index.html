Frequently Asked Questions

Is my donation tax-deductible?

The Nightscout Foundation is a designated 501(c)(3) organization that allows you to make tax deductible donations. Please check with your tax advisor for complete details.

How do monthly donations work? Can I cancel?

Your card is charged the amount you choose once a month, starting today, until you cancel. You can cancel at any time — just contact us and we'll stop future payments.

Where does my donation go?

Your donation will be used to support the programmers around the world who are making a difference in the lives of people with Type 1 Diabetes. Your donation(s) will be used to allow the developers to attend conferences and for providing tools that are needed as they create new solutions for managing diabetes.

Can I volunteer or get more involved?

Volunteers are always welcome to support users of the Nightscout-funded tools through the online support groups. Volunteers are also appreciated when conferences are held around the world for helping developers find their way around. We appreciate you!

Do you accept in-kind donations?

At times, we are asked by developers to provide insulin pumps or other equipment they need for testing purposes. Please keep an eye out through the Facebook support groups and via our irregular newsletters.

Can I donate in honor or in memory of someone?

Yes! After you donate, please contact us with the honoree or memorial name, and the name and email address of the person we should notify.
